How to collect answers
  • One answer per question, with the prompt as given.
  • Greedy decoding with up to 3,072 new tokens.
  • Reasoning models: temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95 and top-k 20, with up to 12,288 tokens.
  • Author responses: a system prompt moves a model's position. Reference and query answers must therefore share a system prompt.
  • Temperature does not move a model's position.
  • Question ids, benchmarks and pool membership: questions.csv.
  • Prompts, gold answers and graders: the code release, data/core/probe.jsonl.
  • The encoder reads the 900 pool questions and ignores the rest. At least 50 must be answered.

    How it works

    • BULLPEN embeds a model from its answers to a fixed probe.
    • Channels: its correctness bits; the questions it got right (correctness-weighted mean question embedding); its own answers (mean BGE answer embedding).
    • Closed-form multi-block PLS-SVD fits loadings per channel. Each channel's scores are z-scored and concatenated.
    • BULLPEN-fused: 12 + 10 + 10 dimensions. BULLPEN-bits+q: 16 + 16 (bits and question text only).

    This demo

    • Fitted with the released code on fold 0, seed 0 of the paper's strict protocol: publisher-grouped folds over models, 900 pool questions.
    • 208 training models, 52 held out.
    • A new model is placed by the paper's fold-in: a ridge solve of its observed bits on the frozen bit loadings, its question block from those bits, and a projection of its mean answer embedding.
    • Every map is linear. The page evaluates them from exported weights; no language model runs here.
    • Heads: the paper's battery readouts (standardised ridge; logistic for base vs chat), refitted once on the training models.
    • Middle view: a PCA of the 260 placements in the selected space. It is linear, so an uploaded model is projected exactly.
    • For density, each axis is scaled to its 2nd to 98th percentile range. Points beyond sit on the edge.
    • Nearest neighbours use cosine similarity in the full 32-d space.

    Limits

    • The submission scores the heads on held-out models from the 260 open-weight models.
    • The author responses add nine closed models placed without refitting (see Reported results).
    • One split and one seed are shown. Per-model values here are single draws; the paper averages over 15 runs.
    • The base-vs-chat label comes from the model name, as in the paper.
